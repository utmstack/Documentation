UTMStack can email your team when a new alert is raised or an incident is created, and it posts system messages, such as rule flood guard warnings, to the notification bell. This page explains who gets what and how to set it up.

## What is sent, and when

| Event | Email | Notification bell |
|---|---|---|
| A new top-level alert is raised | Yes, to **Alert recipients** | No |
| A repeat is filed as an echo, or dropped as a duplicate | No | No |
| An incident is created | Yes, to **Incident recipients** | No |
| Alerts are added to an incident, or an incident is assigned or changes status | No | No |
| The rule flood guard switches a rule off | No | Yes, a **Warning** |

Automated response flows can also send emails and bell notifications. See **Automated Response Overview**.

## Set up email recipients

Email needs a working mail server on **Configure > Settings > Email Configuration** (see **Email Configuration**). You need the Administrator role.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Email Configuration">

Go to **Configure > Settings > Email Configuration**.

</Step>

<Step title="Add alert recipients">

Under **Alert recipients**, add the addresses that should receive new alerts to **To**, and any copies to **CC**. Type an address and click **Add**. The page explains: "Addresses notified when new alerts are raised. Leave empty and alerts are not emailed." Without a **To** address, no alert email is sent.

</Step>

<Step title="Add incident recipients">

Under **Incident recipients**, add addresses to **To** and **CC** in the same way. "Addresses notified when an incident is created. Leave empty and incidents are not emailed." CC alone is not enough: without a **To** address, no incident email is sent.

</Step>

<Step title="Set the organization name and Base URL">

Under **Sender**, the **Organization name** is shown at the start of the subject of alert emails. The **Base URL** is used for the **Open in UTMStack** link in alert emails.

</Step>

<Step title="Save">

Click **Save changes**.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Note>

On a multi-tenant licence each tenant has its own email settings. Every tenant administrator should set the **Alert recipients** and **Incident recipients** of their own tenant. See **Email Configuration**.

</Note>

## What the emails contain

**Alert email**

- Subject: the first 8 characters of the alert ID in brackets, then the alert name: `[<first 8 characters of the alert ID>] <alert name>`. If an organization name is set, it comes first.
- Body: the alert name and description, then Severity, Category, Technique, Data source, Data type, When, and the adversary and target host names when the alert has them.
- An **Open in UTMStack** link to the alert, when a Base URL is set.

Only top-level alerts are emailed. A rule that groups its alerts sends one email for the first alert, not one for each echo, and a duplicate that a rule drops sends none. To get fewer alert emails, add `groupBy` or `deduplicateBy` to noisy rules (see **How Alerts Are Created**).

<Note>

Alerts that a tagging rule closes as **False positive** are still emailed, like any other new top-level alert.

</Note>

**Incident email**

- Subject: `New incident: <incident name>`.
- Body: the incident name, its severity, status and description.

## The notification bell

The bell in the top bar shows unread notifications for your tenant, for example a rule flood guard warning that a rule was switched off. From the bell you can mark notifications as read or unread, delete them, use **Mark all read**, or click **View all** to open **Configure > Settings > Notifications**.

| What you want to do | Permission | Built-in roles |
|---|---|---|
| See notifications | `notifications.read` | Administrator, Analyst, Viewer |
| Mark as read or unread, delete | `notifications.write` | Administrator, Analyst |

Read notifications are deleted after 30 days, and all notifications after 365 days. See **Notifications** for details.
