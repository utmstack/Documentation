On a server with a multi-tenant licence, the instance operator cannot look inside a customer's tenant unless the customer allows it. The tenant's administrator grants this **support access**, and the operator then uses a **support session** to work inside the tenant. This page explains both sides.

## For tenant administrators: grant or take back support access

You need the **Administrator** role in your own tenant. The page exists only on a multi-tenant licence and is not shown to the instance operator.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Support access">

Go to **Configure > Settings > Support access**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose a level">

| Level | What the operator can do |
|---|---|
| **No access** (default) | The operator sees only that your tenant exists. Requests into your data are refused. |
| **Read only** | The operator can look at your alerts, logs and configuration to help you, but cannot change anything. Every change is refused, whatever the operator's own role. |
| **Full access** | The operator can act inside your tenant as an administrator: change configuration, users and response flows. |

</Step>

<Step title="Save">

Click **Save**. The level marked **current** is the one in force.

</Step>

</Steps>

You can change the level or set it back to **No access** at any time; the change applies at once.

<Tip>

Grant **Full access** only while you need hands-on help, and go back to **Read only** or **No access** afterwards.

</Tip>

Everything the operator does inside your tenant is recorded in your tenant's audit log (see **App Audit Logs**).

## For the instance operator: work inside a tenant

You must be the instance operator (see **Settings Overview**), and the tenant must have granted you **Read only** or **Full access**. The tenant card on the Tenants page shows the level each tenant has granted.

### Enter a tenant

Use either of these:

- On the **Tenants** page, click **Enter** on the tenant's card.
- In the top bar, open the tenant switcher (**Switch tenant**) and choose the tenant. The switcher lists only tenants that have granted you access.

A banner at the top of every page then says **You are working inside**, followed by the tenant name and either:

- **read only — writes are refused**, or
- **full access — changes here are real**.

While you are inside a tenant, you see that tenant's data and settings, and the operator-only pages (License, Data Retention, ThreatWinds Integration, About, Tenants) are hidden.

### Leave the tenant

Click **Leave** in the banner, or choose **Default tenant** in the tenant switcher. You are back in your own tenant.

### What to expect

- With **Read only**, you can open pages and searches, but saving, deleting or running anything is refused.
- With **Full access**, your changes are real changes in the customer's tenant. They appear in the customer's audit log under your name.
- If the tenant takes access back while you are inside, your next request is refused.
