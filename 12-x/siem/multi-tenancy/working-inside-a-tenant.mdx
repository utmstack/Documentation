The instance operator can open a customer's tenant and work in it as if signed in there, provided the customer has granted support access. This is called a **support session**. This page explains how to enter and leave a tenant, what the banner and the tenant switcher show, what you can and cannot do at each access level, and what the customer sees in their audit log.

## Who can enter a tenant

All of these must be true:

- The server has a licence with the multi-tenant option.
- You are signed in at the default tenant's address with a user that has the **Administrator** role in the default tenant. Other roles in the default tenant cannot enter customer tenants.
- The customer's administrator has set **Support access** to **Read only** or **Full access**. See **Support Access**.
- The tenant is not deactivated.

The **Tenants** page shows the level each customer has granted on its card: **No access**, **Read access** or **Full access**. Cards of tenants that granted nothing say "This tenant has not granted you access." and have no **Enter** button.

## Enter a tenant

Use either of these:

- On **Tenants**, click **Enter** on the tenant's card, or click anywhere on the card.
- In the top bar, open the tenant switcher (the button with the current tenant's name, **Default tenant** when you are in your own) and choose the tenant. The switcher lists only tenants that granted you **Read only** or **Full access** and are not deactivated.

The page reloads: from the card it opens the tenant's **Overview**; from the switcher it reloads the page you are on. Everything you see from then on is the customer's: their alerts, logs, settings, branding and notifications.

## What changes on screen

![A support session inside a customer tenant: the banner across the top reads You are working inside, the tenant's name and full access — changes here are real, with the Leave button, and the tenant switcher shows the tenant's name](/assets/7ba74663-15f3-4593-a37c-69b4b43c4411)

- A banner across the top of every page says **You are working inside**, followed by the tenant's name, and either **read only — writes are refused** or **full access — changes here are real**. It cannot be closed.
- The tenant switcher shows the tenant's name.
- Pages that belong to the server or to you are hidden: **Tenants**, and in **Settings** the pages **License**, **Data Retention**, **ThreatWinds Integration** and **About**. **Support access** is never shown to you: only the customer can set it.
- The **Apply to all tenants** buttons are hidden.

Your choice is kept in this browser. Tabs of the same browser that you open or reload afterwards also work inside the tenant, until you leave.

## What you can do at each level

| | Read only | Full access |
|---|---|---|
| Open pages and settings | Yes | Yes |
| Alert list, Log Explorer searches, dashboard charts, SOC-AI questions | No in v12.0.2: the server refuses these searches in a read-only session (see the note below) | Yes |
| Save, change, delete, run a flow, change users | No: refused with "this tenant has not granted the platform access" | Yes, with your own permissions (Administrator) |
| Set up integrations and data sources | No: saving is refused | Yes |
| Interactive Console on the tenant's agents | No | No: the console cannot reach a tenant's agents from a support session in v12.0.2 |
| Recorded in the tenant's audit log | Changes are refused, so nothing is changed | Every change, under your email address |

<Note>

In UTMStack v12.0.2 a **Read only** session lets through only plain page reads. The alert list, Log Explorer, dashboard widgets and the SOC-AI assistant send their searches in a way the server treats like a change, so they are refused with "this tenant has not granted the platform access". If you need to investigate a customer's alerts or logs, ask the customer for **Full access** for the time you need it.

</Note>

With **Full access**, your changes are real changes in the customer's tenant. SOC-AI requests you make there count against the tenant's daily AI request cap. See **AI Usage and Limits**.

## When the customer changes the level

The customer can change or take back support access at any time, and the server applies the new level to your next request:

- Raised from **Read only** to **Full access**: your changes are accepted at once.
- Lowered to **Read only**: your changes are refused from now on.
- Set to **No access**: every request is refused with "this tenant has not granted the platform access". Click **Leave**.

The banner keeps showing the level that was granted when you entered. Leave and enter again to see the current one.

If the tenant is suspended or deactivated while you are inside, every request is refused with "this tenant has been suspended or terminated".

## Leave the tenant

Click **Leave** in the banner. You go back to your own tenant's **Tenants** page. You can also choose **Default tenant** in the tenant switcher.

Leave the tenant before you work on your own data again: the banner is the only sign that the data on screen is not yours.

## What the customer sees in their audit log

Every change you make inside a tenant is written to **that tenant's** App Audit Logs, not to yours. The entries look like those of the customer's own users, with your email address as the user. The customer can find them with the **User** filter on **Configure > Settings > App Audit Logs**. Simply opening pages is not recorded. See **App Audit Logs**.

Changes to the tenant itself (creating, editing, deactivating, reactivating and deleting it) are recorded in your own tenant's audit log.

## Work inside a tenant through the API

Programs that act for the instance operator can work inside a tenant by adding the header `X-Tenant-Id` with the tenant's ID to each request. This works only with the access token of a signed-in operator session (header `Authorization: Bearer ...`), sent to the default tenant's address, and only for tenants that granted support access. API keys cannot enter another tenant. See **Authentication and API Keys**.

## Good practice

- Ask the customer for **Full access** only for the time you need it, and agree in writing what you will change.
- Leave the tenant as soon as you are done.
- Do not keep your own tenant open in another tab of the same browser while you work inside a customer's tenant.
