Branding (white-labelling) replaces the UTMStack name, colours and logos with your own. In UTMStack v12 the brand appears on the sign-in screen, in the menu, in emails and in PDF reports. Branding needs an **Enterprise** licence.

## Before you start

- You need the **Administrator** role (`config.write` permission).
- The server needs an Enterprise licence. On Community, the page shows "White-labelling is an Enterprise feature" and nothing can be changed. See **License and Editions**.
- Prepare your images. The page lists PNG, SVG, WEBP, JPG or ICO; GIF is accepted too. Each image can be up to 5 MB. A logo with a transparent background looks best in both the light and the dark theme.

## Set up your brand

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Branding">

Go to **Configure > Settings > Branding**.

![The Branding page with the White-label switch, the Identity section (product name and accent colour) and the Logos section with upload boxes for the light logo, dark logo and favicon](/assets/cc4ef33f-2504-4c3c-8372-c0df7e468217)

</Step>

<Step title="Turn on white-labelling">

Under **White-label**, switch on **Enable white-labeling**.

</Step>

<Step title="Set the identity">

Under **Identity**:

- **Product name**: the name shown in the browser title bar, the menu and emails.
- **Accent color**: the main colour for buttons, links and selected items. Pick one of the eight preset colours or choose **Custom color**.

</Step>

<Step title="Upload logos">

Under **Logos**, for each image click **Upload** (or **Replace**), choose the file, then click **Upload to tenant**:

- **Logo (light)**: used with the light theme.
- **Logo (dark)**: used with the dark theme.
- **Favicon**: the small icon in the browser tab.

</Step>

<Step title="Upload report images">

Under **Report assets**, upload a **Report logo** and a **Report cover** the same way. They are added to the PDF reports UTMStack generates.

</Step>

<Step title="Save">

Click **Save changes**. Sign out to check the sign-in screen with your brand.

</Step>

</Steps>

Each image is uploaded and stored when you click **Upload to tenant** ("Image uploaded"); the other settings are saved with **Save changes** ("Branding updated").

## Go back to the UTMStack look

Click **Restore UTMStack defaults** on the Branding page. This removes your product name, colour and images ("Branding restored to the UTMStack defaults").

To hide your brand only for a while, switch off **Enable white-labeling** and click **Save changes**; your settings and images stay stored.

## Branding and the licence

Your brand is shown only while white-labelling is switched on and the server has a valid Enterprise licence. If the licence expires, UTMStack shows its own look again, and your settings return when a valid licence is installed. See **License and Editions**.

## Troubleshooting

| Message | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The page shows only "White-labelling is an Enterprise feature" | No valid Enterprise licence | Install the licence. |
| "Could not upload image" | The image is larger than 5 MB, or it is not a PNG, JPEG, WEBP, GIF, SVG or ICO file (the server checks the content, not only the name). | Make the image smaller, or export it again in one of the allowed formats. |
| "Could not save branding" | Your roles lack `config.write`, or the licence is not Enterprise | Ask an administrator, or install the licence. |

If the browser tab still shows the old icon after you change the favicon, reload the page while bypassing the cache (for example Ctrl+Shift+R), because browsers keep favicons for a long time.

## Branding and tenants

On a multi-tenant licence, each tenant has its own branding. A tenant administrator can brand their own tenant from the same page. A tenant that has not set up branding shows the standard UTMStack look; it does not take the operator's branding.

## Branding at installation

UTMStack partners can also ship an installer with a built-in brand: a `branding` folder with a `brand.yaml` file and image files, placed next to the installer, sets the product name shown by the installer and the starting branding of the server. A `LICENSE` file placed next to the installer is installed as the licence.
