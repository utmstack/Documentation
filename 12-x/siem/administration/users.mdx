In UTMStack v12 you manage the people who use the product on the **Team** page: you add users, give them roles, and deactivate them when they leave. This page covers users; roles and what each permission allows are described in **Roles and Permissions**.

## Before you start

- Open **Configure > Team**. The **Configure** menu is shown to users with the **Administrator** role.
- The Team page needs these permissions. The **Administrator** role has all of them.

| Permission | Allows |
|---|---|
| `users.read` | List and view users |
| `users.write` | Create and update users, assign their roles, reset their two-step sign-in |
| `users.delete` | Deactivate users |

<Warning>

A user who can create users and assign roles can give any role to anyone, including themselves. Give `users.write` and `roles.write` only to people you would trust with full administrator rights.

</Warning>

## The member list

The **Members** tab lists every user of your organization (your tenant), 20 per page. Search by name or email. The columns are:

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| User | Name and email. An **SSO** badge marks users who sign in through an identity provider. |
| Roles | The roles the user holds |
| 2FA | **On** if the user has turned on two-step sign-in, otherwise **Off** |
| Status | See the table below |

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Active | The user can sign in. |
| Pending invite | The user was invited and has not accepted the invitation yet. |
| Pending password | The account still has its temporary password; the invitation was not completed. |
| Inactive | The account was deactivated and cannot sign in. |
| Suspended | The account is suspended and cannot sign in. |

## Add a user

Click **Invite user**. The dialog has two modes.

| Mode | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Create local user** | You set the password. The account is active at once and no email is sent. |
| **Invite user** | The account is created inactive, with the status **Pending invite**, and UTMStack sends an invitation email to the user. The invitation is valid for 7 days. |

<Tip>

Use **Create local user** when you are setting up a new server, or whenever you are not sure that email works. Invitations need a working mail server and a correct **Base URL** (see **Email Configuration**).

</Tip>

### Create a local user

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the dialog">

Go to **Configure > Team**, open the **Members** tab and click **Invite user**. Choose **Create local user**.

</Step>

<Step title="Fill in the fields">

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Email | The address the user signs in with |
| Name | The user's name |
| Password | A strong password: at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a special character. Spaces are not allowed. |
| Roles | One or more roles. **Viewer** is selected by default. |

</Step>

<Step title="Create the user">

Click **Create user**. Give the user their email address and password through a safe channel, and ask them to change the password from their **Profile**.

</Step>

</Steps>

### Invite a user by email

1. Go to **Configure > Team**, click **Invite user** and keep the **Invite user** mode.
2. Enter the **Email**, the **Name** and the **Roles**.
3. Click **Send invite**. The user appears with the status **Pending invite** until they accept.

## Change a user's roles

1. On the **Members** tab, click the user to open their details.
2. Under **Roles**, select or clear roles. A user can hold more than one role; their permissions are the sum of all of them.
3. Click **Save roles**.

A user with no role has no access to any module.

Role changes reach the user's open sessions within one hour, when their sign-in renews. To apply a change at once, ask the user to sign out and sign in again.

## Reset a user's two-step sign-in

If a user loses access to their authenticator app:

1. Open the user's details on the **Members** tab.
2. Next to **2FA**, click **Reset**, then confirm with **Reset 2FA**.

The user can then sign in with their password and set up two-step sign-in again from their **Profile**. See **Profile and Two-Step Sign-In**.

## Deactivate or reactivate a user

UTMStack never deletes user accounts; you deactivate them instead.

1. Open the user's details on the **Members** tab.
2. Click **Deactivate**. To let the user back in later, open their details again and click **Reactivate**.

When you deactivate a user:

- They cannot sign in, and their sessions can no longer be renewed. A session that is already open stops working within one hour.
- Their API keys stop working at once.

## What you cannot change for a user

The user's name, email and language are edited by the user in their own **Profile**. The user details on the Team page show them read-only.

## Users who sign in with single sign-on

Users of a SAML 2.0 or OpenID Connect identity provider can be created automatically on their first sign-in. They show an **SSO** badge, and their details say "Signs in through an identity provider". They cannot sign in with a local UTMStack password. See **Single Sign-On**.

## Users and tenants

On a multi-tenant licence, users belong to one tenant and sign in on that tenant's address. The same email address can exist in several tenants as separate accounts. Each tenant administrator manages their own tenant's users on their own Team page.
