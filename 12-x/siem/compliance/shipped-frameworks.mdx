UTMStack v12.0.2 ships nine compliance frameworks, all built on one shared library of 320 controls. This page describes what each framework covers, how much of it UTMStack can check from your logs, which log sources settle the automatic checks, and which part always needs a person's verdict. Use it to choose the frameworks you report on and to plan how to raise their scores.

For how controls, statuses and scores work, see **Compliance Overview**. For running and reading a report, see **Compliance Reports**.

| HIPAA Security Rule | 5 | 68 | 78 | 19 | No |

| NIST SP 800-171 Rev 3 | 17 | 97 | 123 | 38 | No |
| NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 | 22 | 106 | 137 | 30 | No |
| PCI DSS v4.0.1 | 14 | 260 | 137 | 39 | No |
| SOC 2 — Trust Services Criteria | 20 | 61 | 95 | 25 | No |

- **Controls used** is how many distinct controls the framework's requirements point to. One control can answer several requirements.
- **Automatic controls** are judged from your logs and alerts. The other controls are manual (governance) controls: policies, training, contracts, physical security and settings that logs cannot show. They stay **Out of scope** until a person records a status, and they keep the score down until then.
- Without an Enterprise licence (the Community edition), only CMMC 2.0 and GDPR can be opened, evaluated and scheduled. See **Compliance Overview** and **License and Editions**.

The frameworks quote official requirement numbers. Where the standard is copyrighted (ISO/IEC 27001, PCI DSS, SOC 2), the requirement titles are short paraphrases and the official text is not reproduced; keep your own copy of the standard for an audit.

## Each framework

### Banking Security Audit

A security-log audit checklist for banks and other financial institutions. It covers what an internal or external auditor checks about log collection and retention, monitoring, real-time alerting, incident evidence, log protection and governance. It is a UTMStack checklist, built from the UTMStack v11 banking audit list and informed by FFIEC, Basel and SWIFT Customer Security Programme expectations; it is not a regulator's text.

It has the highest share of automatic controls (42 of 83), so it is the framework where your logs settle the most.

### GDPR — EU General Data Protection Regulation

The obligations of controllers and processors under Regulation (EU) 2016/679, from the processing principles to international transfers. Security of processing (Article 32) and breach handling (Articles 33 and 34) are checked from logs and alerts; the other obligations, such as lawful basis, data subject rights and the data protection officer, need a person to record their status. Available without an Enterprise licence.

Only 12 of its 50 controls are automatic, so expect a low score until someone records verdicts on the governance requirements.

## The 45 automatic controls

These are the shipped controls that UTMStack judges from your data. All of them use the strategy **ANY**: one passing evidence check is enough, but any failed limit check makes the control **Non-compliant**.

- **Evidence checks** pass when at least one matching record is found, for example "failed logons are recorded".
- **Limit checks** fail when more matching records than allowed are found, for example "an audit log was cleared".

### Controls your team's work settles

Five checks read your alerts rather than your logs. They pass because of what your team does in UTMStack:

| Control | Check | Passes when |
|---|---|---|
| au-6 | Alerts were reviewed (moved to In review or Completed) | At least one alert in the window was set to **In review** or **Completed** |
| ir-4 | Alerts were worked to completion | At least one alert in the window was **Completed** |
| ir-5 | Security alerts were raised and tracked | At least one alert was raised in the window |
| ca-7, si-4 | Security alerts were raised and tracked | Same as ir-5 |

If no alert was raised in a quiet period, ir-5 cannot pass on its own; its remediation text says to set the status by hand and note why. See **Alert Triage Workflow**.

## Raise a framework's score

<Steps>

<Step title="Connect the sources the checks read">

Run the framework once and open the PDF section **Not evaluated — missing sources**. It groups the controls that could not be judged by the log source that would settle them. Connect those sources. See **How Data Reaches UTMStack**.

</Step>

<Step title="Fix what failed">

On the report, open each **Non-compliant** control. **Check results** shows which check failed and how many records matched; the **Remediation** text says what the failure means. Fix the cause, then run the evaluation again. A failed limit check makes the control non-compliant until the window no longer contains the records, so the result clears only after the reporting window has moved past them.

</Step>

<Step title="Work your alerts">

Move alerts to **In review** and **Completed** as you work them, so the alert checks of au-6 and ir-4 pass.

</Step>

<Step title="Record verdicts on manual controls">

Every manual control stays **Out of scope** and counts against the score until a person records a status with a reason, for example where the policy document is kept. See **Compliance Reports**.

</Step>

</Steps>
